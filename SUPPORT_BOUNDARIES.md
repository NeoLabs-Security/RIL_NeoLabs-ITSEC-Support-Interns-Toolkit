# IT Security Support Boundaries

## Default authority

Interns may inspect and document only the assigned training asset or service. Read-only diagnosis is permitted when the ticket and lab instructions authorise access.

## Changes requiring approval

Obtain explicit approval before:

- creating, disabling, deleting or changing an account;
- resetting credentials or MFA;
- changing group membership or permissions;
- opening or closing firewall ports;
- installing or removing software;
- starting, stopping or disabling services;
- changing security, logging, update or backup settings;
- editing scheduled tasks, startup items or system-wide configuration;
- restoring data or rolling back a system.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- disabling antivirus, firewall, logging, encryption or access controls merely to resolve a symptom;
- clearing logs or deleting suspicious artefacts;
- using shared administrator credentials;
- copying real user data into personal storage or public repositories;
- making unrecorded changes;
- working on an unassigned asset;
- presenting a successful command as proof that the root cause is understood.

## Incident indicators

Pause routine support and escalate when evidence suggests:

- unauthorised account or privilege changes;
- malware or suspicious persistence;
- unusual authentication activity;
- unexpected data access or transfer;
- security-control tampering;
- unexplained log loss;
- compromise of an administrator or service account.

## Change standard

Every approved change must record:

1. original state;
2. reason and expected outcome;
3. risk and dependencies;
4. approval;
5. implementation steps;
6. validation evidence;
7. rollback method;
8. final status and handover.
